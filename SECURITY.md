# Security policy

## You broke out of an escape room

Claim it in the console first, so the receipt carries your hotkey. Then send
us the method in private: open the Security tab of
[cathedral-sandbox](https://github.com/cathedralai/cathedral-sandbox/security)
and choose **Report a vulnerability**.

Please include the room id, the steps or commands, and what you saw.

We confirm receipt, reproduce the issue, fix it, and credit you when the
advisory is published, unless you ask us not to. Do not publish a working
method before the fix ships.

## Anything else

Use the same private form for any security issue in Cathedral. Do not open a
public issue.
